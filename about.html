Creator & Genesis Story

Designed by a Software Engineering Student

CpShare TXT was created, designed, and developed by Kavindu Bogahawatte. At the time of inception, Kavindu was a dedicated 1st year undergraduate Software Engineering student in Sri Lanka.

Observing the repeated frustration of peers struggling to transfer code snippets, SQL queries, and lecture summaries during campus lab practicals without exposing their personal WhatsApp or Google accounts on shared workstations, he architected an ephemeral, zero-login sharing utility.

1st Deployed Live Project — December 2024
Officially launched into production in December 2024 as his maiden web engineering release.
